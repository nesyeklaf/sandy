Hand your backlog to a chief of staff. Each item gets its own agent thread,
routine questions get answered without you, and you see only progress and
the decisions that are yours to make.

## What you get

- A **Chief of staff** page in the sidebar: paste a backlog, pick a project,
  and the items start, a few at a time.
- A chief-of-staff thread that answers the workers' routine questions, using
  your standing guidance and your earlier decisions as precedent.
- **Decisions for you**: questions only you can decide, with the reason, the
  options, and a recommendation. Answer with one click; the worker continues.
- A briefing: what is done, in progress, queued, or failed, the latest report
  from each item, and an activity feed.
- `sandy chief-of-staff brief | list | decisions | add | decide | pause | resume`.

## How it works

Everything runs inside SANDY: worker and chief threads are ordinary threads in
your project, and the plugin keeps its state in its own local database. No
account or external service is needed.
